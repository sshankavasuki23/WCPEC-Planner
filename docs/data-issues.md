## Missing or partial source details

- Individual slots are absent for the six talks in the two-speaker silicon, PV modules and tandem tutorials (pp. 55–61). Their session windows remain available.
- Poster codes identify presentations. The supplied PDF lists DCC 2, Exhibition Hall and a shared 18:00–19:00 window on Monday, Tuesday and Thursday (pp. 24–25). Individual board-to-position mappings are unavailable in that PDF.
- The ending of the printed title for `4BO.3-OP-5` appears incomplete on p. 105, ending at “Ultrathin”. The planner preserves the words shown.
- The Tuesday Next-Generation BIPV Forum and the Wednesday Energy Talk Concert entries appear as scheduled events. The relevant PDF tables do not provide individual presentation metadata for those entries. Generic “Special Session” blocks in Thursday’s Room 7 timetable and the Tuesday “Energy” block in the overview lack a resolved title/time-to-room association as standalone items. The named sessions with detailed evidence are shown separately.
- Some uncoded welcomes, closing remarks, breaks and panel segments are represented through the parent workshop window. Unspecified authors or speakers are shown as missing when an individual row is included.
- Room typography varies between PDF tables, for example `Session Room 1(101-102)` and `Session Room 1 (101-102)`. The displayed strings retain the table’s wording while the room filter groups the two forms together.
- The organiser’s online programme did not provide a readable record in the verification environment. Confirm the flagged records against organiser updates if using those entries to attend.
